How to play

  • Swipe a block (or tap it, then an arrow). It glides across the ice until something stops it.
  • Dock every coloured block. A block that slides into a gate of its own colour leaves the board. Other gates act like walls.
  • Paint pads recolour any block that glides over them.
  • Stones never dock — but they make great stoppers.
  • Portals carry a block to the matching portal on the opposite edge.

Keyboard: Tab select · ←↑→↓ slide · Z undo · R restart · H hint
